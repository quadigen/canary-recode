---
title: Water
description: Configure the waves and surface appearance of water in the world.
---

Water is a settings object for the engine's water system. It does not create a water volume by itself. Use a Part with the Water material or water terrain to provide the liquid surface.

```lua
local water = Instance.new("Water")
water.Speed = 1.5
water.Strength = 0.8
water.FoamAmount = 0.4
water.Parent = workspace
```

The water physics settings use the last enabled, parented Water object. Use one active settings object when you want predictable global wave behavior.

## Properties

All properties are read/write.

| Property | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enabled | true | Enables this settings object. |
| Speed | 1 | Wave speed, clamped to 0–10. |
| Strength | 1 | Wave strength, clamped to 0–10. |
| Octaves | 6 | Wave detail, clamped to 1–32. |
| Height | 1.75 | Wave height setting, clamped to 0–10. |
| Adornee | nil | Stored instance reference; does not restrict the global physics settings to that object. |
| Roughness | 0.08 | Surface roughness, 0–1. |
| IOR | 1.333 | Index of refraction, 1–3. |
| Thickness | 0.12 | Optical thickness, 0–1. |
| Transmission | 0.88 | Light transmission, 0–1. |
| FoamAmount | 0.55 | Foam amount, 0–1. |
| ClearCoat | 1 | Clear coat amount, 0–1. |
| ClearCoatRoughness | 0.04 | Clear coat roughness, 0–1. |
| Absorption | Color3.new(0.35, 0.09, 0.04) | RGB absorption coefficients. |

Numeric optical settings are clamped to the indicated ranges. Water has no additional methods or events. See [Terrain](/docs/services/Terrain) and [Part](/docs/Part) for creating water geometry.

## Surface appearance

`Roughness` controls how sharp reflections look. `IOR`, `Transmission`, and `Thickness` affect how light passes through the surface, while `Absorption` changes which colors fade through the water. `FoamAmount` controls the foam around the waves.

Wave speed and strength affect the animated surface and water behavior. You can change the optical properties independently of the wave settings.

The renderer uses animated wave shading and foam, but `Water` doesn't simulate a full ocean. Add actual water geometry to see the result, and test your settings in the full rendered viewport.
