---
title: SphereHandleAdornment
description: Draw a wireframe sphere at a part.
---

`SphereHandleAdornment` draws a sphere centered on its target part. Use it to mark a radius or a point of interest.

## Getting started

```lua
local sphere = Instance.new("SphereHandleAdornment")
sphere.Adornee = targetPart
sphere.Radius = 6
sphere.Color3 = Color3.fromRGB(80, 200, 255)
sphere.Parent = targetPart
```

## Properties

| Property | Type | Access | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Adornee` | `Instance?` | Read/write | `nil` | Part to center on. Falls back to the parent. |
| `Color3` | `Color3` | Read/write | Green | Wire color. |
| `Radius` | `number` | Read/write | `1` | Sphere radius in studs. |

## Notes

- Inherits from `ValueBase`, which adds no members.
- The sphere is centered on the part's position, not on its bounding box.
- If `Adornee` is `nil`, the parent is used, and the parent must be a `Part`.
- Cloning copies the target, color, and radius.

## See also
