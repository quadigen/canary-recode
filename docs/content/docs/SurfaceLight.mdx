---
title: SurfaceLight
description: Light an area outward from one face of a part.
---

`SurfaceLight` emits from the surface of a part face rather than from a point. It's useful for glowing panels, screens, and ceiling fixtures.

## Getting started

```lua
local light = Instance.new("SurfaceLight")
light.Face = Enum.NormalId.Bottom
light.Angle = 120
light.Range = 24
light.Brightness = 2
light.Parent = ceilingPanel
```

## Properties

| Property | Type | Access | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Brightness` | `number` | Read/write | `1` | Light strength, clamped to `0` or above. |
| `Color` | `Color3` | Read/write | White | Light color. |
| `Enabled` | `boolean` | Read/write | `true` | Whether the light is emitted. |
| `Shadows` | `boolean` | Read/write | `false` | Whether the light casts shadows. |
| `Angle` | `number` | Read/write | `90` | Spread angle in degrees, clamped to `0` through `180`. |
| `Face` | `Enum.NormalId` | Read/write | `Front` | Face of the part the light emits from. |
| `Range` | `number` | Read/write | `16` | Falloff distance in studs, clamped to `0` or above. |

## Notes

- The light origin sits on the chosen face, at the face's center offset by half the part size.
- Unlike `SpotLight`, the emitter is placed on the surface, which suits panels and signs.
- The parent must be a `Part` inside the workspace.
- Cloning copies the angle, face, range, and shared light settings.

## See also

- `SpotLight` for a light pulled back to the part center.
- `PointLight` for omni-directional light.
- `Light` for the shared settings.
