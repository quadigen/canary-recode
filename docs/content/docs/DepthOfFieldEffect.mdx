---
title: DepthOfFieldEffect
description: Blur the foreground and background around a chosen focus distance.
---

`DepthOfFieldEffect` keeps a band of the scene sharp and blurs what's closer to or farther from the camera. It's useful for close-up shots, cutscenes, and making a busy background recede.

`DepthOfFieldEffect` inherits from `LightingEffect`, so it has `Enabled`. It must be a child of `Lighting`.

## Getting started

`Lighting` creates a disabled depth-of-field effect by default. Find it, or add one, then enable it:

```lua
local Lighting = game:GetService("Lighting")

local dof = Lighting:FindFirstChild("DepthOfFieldEffect")
if not dof then
    dof = Instance.new("DepthOfFieldEffect")
    dof.Parent = Lighting
end

dof.Enabled = true
dof.FocusDistance = 24
dof.InFocusRadius = 10
dof.NearIntensity = 0.35
dof.FarIntensity = 0.6
```

## Properties

| Property | Type | Access | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Enabled` | `boolean` | Read/write | `true` | Turns the effect on or off. |
| `FarIntensity` | `number` | Read/write | `0.25` | Blur strength behind the focus band, clamped between `0` and `1`. |
| `FocusDistance` | `number` | Read/write | `10` | Distance from the camera to the sharp area. Clamped to at least `0.001`. |
| `InFocusRadius` | `number` | Read/write | `10` | Size of the sharp band around `FocusDistance`. Negative values clamp to `0`. |
| `NearIntensity` | `number` | Read/write | `0.25` | Blur strength in front of the focus band, clamped between `0` and `1`. |

The effect turns on when `Enabled` is true and at least one of `NearIntensity` or `FarIntensity` is greater than `0`. Setting both to `0` is the same as disabling it.

## Tuning it

`FocusDistance` is measured in studs from the camera. A large `InFocusRadius` makes almost everything sharp; a small one narrows the sharp area and makes the blur more obvious.

`NearIntensity` and `FarIntensity` are quantized to roughly 32 steps for rendering, so extremely small changes at the low end may not be visible. They're also clamped to `0`-`1`; values above `1` don't add more blur.

If the camera moves, update `FocusDistance` from the distance to your subject. Reading the camera's `CFrame` and subtracting the subject position is enough:

```lua
local camera = workspace.CurrentCamera

local function focusOn(subjectPosition: Vector3)
    local distance = (subjectPosition - camera.CFrame.Position).Magnitude
    dof.FocusDistance = distance
end
```

## Notes

- Only one depth-of-field effect is used. The last `DepthOfFieldEffect` child of `Lighting` wins.
- The effect is skipped in the editor's fast viewport.
- Cloning copies `Enabled` and all four tuning values.
