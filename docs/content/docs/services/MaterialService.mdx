---
title: MaterialService
description: The material service container.
---

MaterialService is a built-in service that you can obtain from the DataModel:

```lua
local materials = game:GetService("MaterialService")
print(materials.Name)
```

The service has no material-specific Luau properties, methods, or events beyond its inherited object API. It does not expose material creation, lookup, or override methods.

Use [MaterialVariant](/docs/MaterialVariant) and the material properties on [Part](/docs/Part) to configure surface appearance. See the [lighting and effects guide](/docs/guides/lighting-and-effects) for related visual settings.
